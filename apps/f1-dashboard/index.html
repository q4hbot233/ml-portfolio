The two models that need more than one race

Lap-time prediction and the anomaly flag are questions inside a session, and one race is enough data for them. These two are different: when a team pits, and how fast a compound wears, depend on circuits, conditions and rules. Fit either on a single race and you have measured that race. Both are fitted on —, split by season rather than at random: a random split puts the 2023 and 2024 running of the same circuit on opposite sides, and scores the model on a track it has already learned.
