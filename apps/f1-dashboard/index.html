The two models that need more than one race

Lap-time prediction and the anomaly flag are questions inside a session, and one race is the right substrate for them. These two are not. When does a team pit and how fast does a compound fall away are questions about circuits, conditions and rules — fit either on a single race and you have measured that race. Both are fitted on —, split by season rather than at random: a random split puts the 2023 and 2024 running of the same circuit on opposite sides, and scores the model on a track it has already learned.
