Mortgage lending · partial identification

The variable that was left out

In short297,104 Georgia mortgage applications from 2023 that reached an approve-or-deny decision. A LightGBM model predicts denial with an average precision of 0.84, so what it has learned is the lenders' policy. Two gaps limit what the data can say about race. Lenders name credit history in 30.5% of denials, and the file has no credit score. And 91,321 applications were withdrawn or left incomplete: if those are allowed to differ from the decided ones by about 34%, the data can no longer say whether Black or white applicants were denied more often.
